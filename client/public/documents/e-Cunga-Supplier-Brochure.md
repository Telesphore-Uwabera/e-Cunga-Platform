  SECTION 1 — WHY JOIN e-CUNGA AS A SUPPLIER?
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

clinics, hotels, retailers, government institutions, and other

  SECTION 3 — EIGHT FEATURES BUILT FOR SUPPLIERS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. REQUEST & PROFORMAS INBOX
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

When a buyer's supervisor forwards a purchase requisition to you,
it lands instantly in your inbox.

  What you see for each request:
  • Items needed — name, quantity, unit
  • Requesting location and department
  • Estimated budget from the buyer
  • Supervisor notes and any special requirements
  • Priority level (Normal / High / Critical)

  What you do:
  1. Review the request details in full.
  2. Click "Submit proforma" — upload your proforma PDF and
     enter your unit prices and total amount.
  3. The buyer's accountant receives it automatically for review.
  4. You are notified when the proforma is approved or rejected.

  All requests are searchable. Filter by status:
  • Action required → needs your proforma
  • With finance → proforma under accountant review
  • Paid — dispatch → approved, payment done, ship now
  • Closed → completed cycle

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  2. INVOICE MANAGEMENT (Documents)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every order has a full document trail linked to it — proforma,
delivery note, and official final invoice.

  Invoice statuses you will see:

  Status                  Meaning
  ─────────────────────── ─────────────────────────────────────
  Proforma Received        Buyer received your proforma
  Proforma Approved        Approved — proceed to deliver
  Delivery Note Attached   Buyer confirmed delivery
  Final Invoice Received   Your invoice received by buyer
  Partially Paid           Partial payment has been made
  Credit Purchase          Sold on agreed credit terms
  Paid                     Full payment received ✓
  Closed                   Cycle complete, archived ✓

  Actions on each invoice:
  • Upload your official delivery note PDF
  • Upload your final invoice PDF
  • Track payment progress bar (amount paid vs total)
  • See payment channel used (bank transfer, mobile money, etc.)
  • View payment proof uploaded by the buyer's accountant

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3. PRODUCT CATALOG (Marketplace Presence)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Your catalog is your shopfront on e-Cunga. Buyers search by
category when placing orders and compare pricing across suppliers.

  Adding a product takes under 2 minutes:
  • Product name and description
  • Category (Medical, Office, F&B, Maintenance, etc.)
  • Unit (pieces, kg, boxes, litres, etc.)
  • Unit price in RWF
  • Product images (stored securely on Cloudinary)
  • Availability status

  Cunga AI monitors category demand signals from buyer
  requisitions across the platform. It surfaces tips like:
  "Save copy and pricing before publishing — Cunga AI tips
  react to category demand signals."

  Your catalog count is tracked in your dashboard KPIs and
  in the Cunga AI supplier insight card.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4. DELIVERY MANAGEMENT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Track all approved, paid orders waiting for physical delivery.

  Delivery workflow:
  1. Order moves to "Paid — dispatch" status on your dashboard.
  2. Prepare the goods for delivery.
  3. Upload delivery note PDF in the portal.
  4. Buyer confirms receipt — status becomes "Delivery Note
     Attached."
  5. Submit your final official invoice PDF.

  Cunga AI Delivery Insight:
  When multiple paid orders are going to the same buyer location,
  Cunga AI surfaces corridor consolidation opportunities:
  "When paid orders land in your queue, Cunga AI will surface
  corridor consolidation opportunities and estimated savings here."
  This helps you batch deliveries to the same site and reduce
  transport costs.

  SECTION 4 — HOW THE BUYER-SUPPLIER WORKFLOW WORKS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  BUYER SIDE                        YOUR SIDE (SUPPLIER)
  ─────────────────────────────     ──────────────────────────────
  1. Clerk identifies low stock     ←  Your items are in demand
     and raises a requisition

  2. Supervisor approves and        ←  You receive the RFQ in
     forwards to you                   your inbox

  3. You review items needed        →  You submit proforma PDF
                                       with your prices

  4. Accountant reviews             ←  You are notified of
     proforma                          approval or rejection

  5. Proforma approved              →  You prepare and ship goods

  6. You upload delivery note       →  Buyer confirms receipt

  7. You upload final invoice       →  Accountant processes payment

  8. Payment processed              ←  You see payment in portal
                                       (amount, method, proof)

  9. Order closed                   ←  Archived for your records

  Every step is timestamped. Every document stays attached.
  No phone calls. No lost emails. Full audit trail.

  SECTION 9 — YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Q: Is there a cost to join as a supplier?
  A: Supplier registration on e-Cunga is free. Buyers pay for
     their workspace plan; suppliers access the platform at no
     charge to submit proformas and process orders.

  Q: How do I know when a new order arrives?
  A: You receive an in-app notification and an email the moment
     a supervisor forwards a request to you. The request also
     appears immediately in your inbox on the portal.

  Q: Can I reject or counter a purchase request?
  A: Yes. If you cannot fulfil a request at the requested price
     or quantity, you can submit a proforma with revised terms.
     The accountant and supervisor will review it. You can also
     message the supervisor directly to discuss before submitting.

  Q: How long does payment take after I submit my invoice?
  A: Payment timing depends on the buyer organisation's payment
     policy. The portal sends automatic reminders to the buyer's
     accountant at 7 days and 1 day before the payment deadline,
     reducing delays.

  Q: Can I see which buyers are looking at my products?
  A: Your catalog is visible in the Marketplace. Cunga AI
     provides insight cards on your catalog's performance and
     demand signals in your category — without exposing
     individual buyer identities.

  Q: What file formats can I upload for invoices?
  A: PDF is recommended for proformas, delivery notes, and
     final invoices. Images (JPG, PNG) are also accepted.

  Q: Is the portal available in Kinyarwanda?
  A: Yes. Switch between English and Kinyarwanda using the
     ENG / KINY buttons in the top bar. Cunga AI insights are
     also generated in Kinyarwanda when selected.
